The reality

Running a café shouldn't feel like running five businesses.

Most cafés juggle sales on one system, inventory in a spreadsheet, waste on paper, and reports somewhere else. The result is disconnected data and decisions made on gut feeling.

📊

Disconnected sales

Cash register in one place, totals in another, no view of what's actually selling.

📝

Spreadsheet inventory

Stock counts that are always a week behind. Reorders happen after you've run out.

🗑️

Invisible waste

Food goes in the bin, but nobody knows which products or what it costs.

📉

Reports that arrive late

Monthly numbers show what already went wrong — not what to do next.

The solution

One workspace. Every part of your café.

CafeCash brings sales, inventory, recipes, waste, suppliers, and reports into a single connected system — then adds an intelligence layer that tells you what's happening and what to do about it.

    Understand, not just track

Security architecture

Your business data, protected.

CafeCash uses Firebase Authentication for identity and enforces café-level data isolation at the backend, so each business's data stays separate.

  • 🔐 Firebase Authentication with email & password
  • 🛡️ Server-side authorization on every request
  • 🏪 Café-level data isolation enforced technically
  • 🔑 Role-based permissions for team access
  • 📋 Session validation and secure token handling
  • 🚨 Firestore security rules and app protections
